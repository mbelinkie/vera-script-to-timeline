## Authority conflict and open Producer decision

#58 promises that an update starts from a duplicate of the current timeline and
keeps existing Resolve work, but its accepted prototype does not identify
protected or incompatible Resolve work and has no partial-update result model.
The Product Spec says unprovable attachment is a conflict, prior timelines stay
authoritative, selected results must verify before a new baseline, and partial
failures remain retryable. The pilot therefore does not invent silent
preservation or checkbox-based resolution. It asks whether the high-level
successor should:

1. block only the affected update with a compact `Needs review in Resolve`
   result while allowing independent safe updates; or
2. block the whole update plan until the protected/incompatible item is
   reviewed.

This choice is outside the five ordinary mechanics and must be settled by the
Producer before full assembly.

## Renderer reuse boundary

The accepted #135 export is a Claude bundle whose `text/x-dc` source contains
one coupled editor component. The bounded private adaptation copies only the
ordinary row CSS/tokens and the row, narration-range, visual-card and music-rail
rendering patterns. The pilot supplies immutable scenario records and explicit
annotations, and replaces mutation callbacks with read-only inspection
callbacks. The full editor is not mounted, framed or hidden. The accepted file
is never edited. A check records its exact hash and the copied-source markers.

If the pilot proves that faithful range/card behavior cannot be retained within
this boundary, stop before expansion with a measured extraction plan; do not
silently rebuild a lookalike.
