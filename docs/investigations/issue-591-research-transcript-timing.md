# Research transcript timing for a logged clip

Source findings for [Timeline #591](https://github.com/mbelinkie/vera-script-to-timeline/issues/591). The live one-clip check is pending. The documented loopback Research API had no listener, and the Timeline CLI stopped at its missing-bearer check. The findings below describe the linked source revisions.

| Path | Timing available |
| --- | --- |
| Research clip list and detail | One start and end for the logged transcript selection, plus separate export bounds, all in integer milliseconds. The response includes the first and last segment IDs, optional first and last token IDs, and selection precision (`word`, `cue`, or `estimated`). A caption-derived end can be marked `endTiming: "derived"`. The selection contains no segment or word intervals. |
| Timeline clip snapshot | Keeps those bounds, IDs, text, and the selection precision. The snapshot omits the separate `endTiming` marker. The clip client makes no transcript request, and the snapshot has no segment or word intervals. |
| Research published English transcript | A separate authorized read serves the active transcript. Its segments have start and end milliseconds. Tokens may have acoustic start and end, caption-reported start with a derived end, or no bounds. An unaligned token has no bounds. The active transcript might differ from the version logged on the clip. |

**For S03.** The logged selection alone supports the full-transcript fallback in D150. It cannot determine which words survive an inward trim, whether the trim has no speech, or where the D163 brackets should snap. A matching timed transcript with segments can support D150's overlapping-cue excerpt, with uncertainty at each cut of up to the duration of the cue it crosses. D178's exact inside words and D163's verified word stops need reliable word intervals at the affected boundaries. A `word` precision flag on a clip describes its outer selection bounds. Millisecond units alone say nothing about acoustic accuracy. [S03 decisions](../prototypes/issue-125/r29/S03%20design%20decisions.md)

**Follow-up for [Timeline #83](https://github.com/mbelinkie/vera-script-to-timeline/issues/83).** Reuse the authorized Research transcript when its track and version match the clip. Preserve timing status and carry only the cues and tokens needed for that logged selection into Timeline's snapshot. If the active read cannot serve the logged version, request a version-pinned, clip-scoped read from Research. Where acoustic timings are absent, use Research's existing opt-in alignment path (Research #47, built on Timeline #183's shared package) and retain the cue or full-transcript fallback for unaligned text. Research #47 measured **3.96 minutes per audio hour** on the Intel Mac with generated audio, caption handoff, audio preparation, VAD and four-job MFA. Fresh recognition throughput and end-to-end cost for the logged clip are unmeasured.
