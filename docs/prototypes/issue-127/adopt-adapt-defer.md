evidence, not authority. `Adopt`, `Adapt`, and `Defer` record the decisions
accepted for the bounded successor; they do not change a product contract.

## Decision boundary

The Producer selected persistent numbering, clarified that visual ordinals
reset per row, and chose a separate M namespace for music. Minimal and the first
global-numbered successor remain rejected evidence. The corrected successor
implements the rules above, but none becomes wider design authority until the
focused Claude critique is reconciled and the Producer accepts the exact
artifact.
